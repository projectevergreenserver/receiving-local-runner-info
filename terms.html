Receiving Local Runner: use and access

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

This private desktop application is maintained for its owner's authorized receiving work. It is under development and is not offered as a public service or download.

The owner controls its Google authorization, local installation and access to business systems. The application must follow the owner's receiving procedures, retain purchase receipts on hold and leave release decisions to the owner. Unresolved source facts, access failures and uncertain saved outcomes require reconciliation.

Google services and the ERP remain subject to their applicable terms. This information website does not offer a subscription, collect payments or create a service commitment.

Questions can be directed to the user-support contact shown on the application's Google consent screen.
