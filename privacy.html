Receiving Local Runner privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Data accessed and purpose

Receiving Local Runner accesses Google Drive file metadata and invoice documents, and Google Sheets invoice forms and receiving reports. Those records may include supplier and customer details, invoice identifiers, products, quantities, prices and receiving annotations. It uses this information to discover and interpret invoices, reconcile existing work, prepare purchase receipts for review, and create or update designated forms and reports when those operations are implemented and validated.

Permissions

Google authorization permits reading and downloading Drive files, managing Drive files used with this app, and reading and editing spreadsheets. These Google permissions cover more than the configured receiving folders. The controller separately limits its operations to designated sources, forms and reports. It does not use these permissions to share documents or delete or move source invoices.

Storage and processing

Downloaded evidence, extracted records, checkpoints, action logs and OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's computer. Runtime credential files are restricted to the local operating-system user and excluded from the project's version control. Routine model processing uses the owner's local model. Google API requests send the identifiers, reads and authorized document changes necessary for the workflow to Google. ERP entry sends the intended receipt information to the owner's configured ERP.

The standalone controller does not send invoice content to a hosted language-model provider, sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train a general-purpose model. If the owner separately asks a support or development service to inspect data, that service's processing is separate from the standalone controller and subject to the owner's instructions and that service's terms.

Retention and control

Local evidence and logs remain until the owner removes them. Revoking access stops future authorized Google requests but does not delete local copies or records already saved in Google or the ERP. The owner can revoke the connection in Google Account permissions and remove the local credentials and working data. Removing receiving checkpoints can impair duplicate prevention and resumption, so retained business records should be reconciled before deletion.

Contact and changes

The application is maintained for its owner's use. Use the support contact shown on its Google consent screen for privacy questions. Material changes to data access or processing must be reflected here and in the application's authorization before use.
