Receiving Local Runner

A private desktop application for its owner's receiving workflow. It connects designated Google Drive invoice folders and Google Sheets forms and reports to a locally operated purchase-receipt workflow.

Development status: Google file discovery and spreadsheet reads are implemented. The complete unattended receiving workflow is still under development. This page is application information; it does not provide access to the desktop application or business documents.

Google access

The application requests permission to read and download Drive files, manage Drive files used with the app, and read and edit spreadsheets. Google grants these permissions more broadly than individual folders. The application limits its operations to its configured receiving sources, invoice forms and reports.

When implemented and validated, form creation and report updates use those permissions. Purchase receipts remain on hold for the owner's review.

Local operation

The controller, its working files and its routine language model run on the owner's computer. This information website does not run the controller, accept invoices, or provide remote control. The application is for its owner, with no public sign-up or distribution.

For questions, use the user-support contact displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
